Daily Reps — Clean Voice Test

Beta test only, not the live site. Voice only, no music. Pauses: 5 seconds between practice segments, 3 seconds before the reflection question. No “We will practise again tomorrow” in any Rep — practise again tomorrow, or not, is the listener’s option.

Close your eyes for each Rep. After the question, write one line in your own words.

1. Body Scan — Notice

Question: Today, which part of your body felt the tightest?

2. Naming — Give It a Name

Question: Today, what name did you give that feeling?

3. Sealing — Note It Down, Put It Away

Question: Today, which passage did you want to seal for now?

4. Senses — Back to the Present

Question: Today, which small thing brought you back to the present?

Note: for this one, keep your eyes open.

5. Focus — One Thing at a Time

Question: Today, what was the one small thing you focused on?

中文版 — 干净人声

中文版与英文版结构相同：纯人声，无音乐，段间 5 秒停顿，问题前 3 秒，无“明天再练”那句。声音方向：柔软、空灵、温柔的成年女声，仅为风格参考，不复制任何真人声音。

1. 身体扫描 — 觉察

问题：今天，你身体的哪个部位最紧？

2. 命名 — 给它一个名字

问题：今天，你给那个感受起了什么名字？

3. 封存 — 写下来，放进盒子

问题：今天，你想先封存哪一段话？

4. 感官 — 回到当下

问题：今天，是哪个小东西把你带回了当下？

注：这一段保持眼睛睁开。

5. 专注 — 一次只做一件事

问题：今天，你专注的那件小事是什么？
