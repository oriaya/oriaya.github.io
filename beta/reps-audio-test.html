Daily Reps

Five short guided practices. Close your eyes and listen — each one takes two to three minutes. Each begins with a short introduction, then a few cues with silence in between.

When a practice ends, answer the question in one line, in your own words. That is the whole practice.

1. Body Scan — Notice (2:42)

Which part of your body felt the tightest today?

2. Naming — Give It a Name (2:58)

What name did you give that feeling today?

3. Sealing — Note It Down, Put It Away (3:04)

Which passage did you want to seal for now?

4. Senses — Back to the Present (3:29)

Which small thing brought you back to the present?

For this one, keep your eyes open.

5. Focus — One Thing at a Time (3:07)

What was the one small thing you focused on today?

每日练习

五个简短的引导练习。闭上眼睛，跟着声音走，每个两到三分钟。每个练习先有一段简短引导，然后是几句提示，中间留白。

练完之后，回答下面那个问题，用自己的话写下一行。这就是整个练习。

1. 身体扫描 — 觉察 (2:46)

今天，你身体的哪个部位最紧？

2. 命名 — 给它一个名字 (3:07)

今天，你给那个感受起了什么名字？

3. 封存 — 写下来，放进盒子 (3:03)

今天，你想先封存哪一段话？

4. 感官 — 回到当下 (3:12)

今天，是哪个小东西把你带回了当下？

这一段保持眼睛睁开。

5. 专注 — 一次只做一件事 (2:47)

今天，你专注的那件小事是什么？
