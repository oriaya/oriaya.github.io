Daily practice

Daily Reps

Daily Reps, the Mind Reps in the app, are five short practices. Each one takes 3 to 5 minutes, in chat or audio. Small on their own, they add up: a few minutes a day trains your mind the way short sets train a muscle.

Rep 01

Body Scan

Slowly notice your body, from head to toe, and where you hold tension. It trains attention and brings you back to the present moment.

Rep 02

Naming

Name what you feel, in plain words. It trains clarity: once a feeling has a name, it is easier to meet it calmly.

Rep 03

Sealing

Pause at the end of a moment and let a good feeling settle and stay, instead of rushing past it. It trains the mind to hold on to what is steady.

Rep 04

Senses

Notice what you see, hear, smell, taste, and touch, one sense at a time. It trains presence: your attention lands where your body is.

Rep 05

Focus

Hold your attention on one thing, like your breath, and bring it back gently when it wanders. It trains focus and self-command.

Why it works

The idea comes from Positive Intelligence, or PQ: the mind's ability to meet a challenge with a positive mindset instead of a negative one. PQ grows through short reps, not long lectures. Each practice is one small rep for self-command: the ability to pause, choose your response, and return to calm. A few minutes a day is enough to begin.

The core of Oriaya

Deep Sessions

A Deep Session is a 15 to 30 minute conversation with Ria, the AI coach who remembers you. She picks up where you left off, so you never start over and never repeat yourself.

Deep Sessions are the heart of Oriaya because change happens in conversation. Being seen, heard, and remembered is what lets a small shift become a real one. The practices train the mind. The conversation is where it grows.

A session starts with a check-in: how you are, in your own words. Then you explore, one step at a time, while Ria asks questions and holds space. When the time feels right, you write your own takeaway and one small action. Ria only polishes your words. The meaning stays yours.

Your takeaways are saved, so you can look back and see how you have grown, session by session. One free Deep Session comes with every account, so you can try it before you decide anything.

The basics

What coaching is

A coach does not give you answers. A coach asks questions, listens closely, and helps you see your own thinking clearly enough to act on it. The work is yours. The coach walks beside you while you do it.

It is coaching, not therapy. Therapy often looks back to heal. Coaching looks forward to grow. If you are in crisis, please reach out to a crisis line or a therapist. You can find numbers in the app.

At the close of a session, Ria offers the ABC cards: three short cards that gather what shifted. The last card ends with one line: 'You are the creator of your own life.'

You are the creator of your own life.

Some moments call for a real person. Oriaya offers 60-minute one-to-one sessions with a human coach, online or in person near Palo Alto. Sessions are led by International Certified Coach (ICF) professional life and executive coaches.

From the lab

Emotion regulation, from the lab

Emotion regulation is a simple idea with deep roots: it is how we notice, understand, and shape the way we respond to what we feel. The Stanford Psychophysiology Lab studies how people do this in everyday life: what helps, what gets in the way, and how a small shift in attention can change the course of a feeling.

As a Visiting Researcher in the lab, I am learning this science firsthand, and I will bring what I learn into Oriaya. This section will grow as I learn. More is coming.
